What we do

Auto repair & maintenance services

From routine upkeep to "it's making a noise I don't like," Wrench and Soul handles the everyday repairs that keep Brantley County drivers on the road.

Brakes & Rotors

Grinding, squealing or a soft pedal? We inspect pads, rotors and lines and get your stopping power back where it should be.

Oil Changes & Maintenance

Oil and filter changes, fluid checks and top-offs, belts, hoses and filters — the simple upkeep that prevents expensive repairs.

Engine Diagnostics & Check Engine Light

We read the codes, then look past them. We track down why the light is on and explain what it means for your vehicle.

Suspension & Steering

Shocks and struts, ball joints, tie rods and bushings. If your ride is bouncing or wandering, we'll tighten it up.

Batteries, Starters & Alternators

Slow crank, dead dash, or a click when you turn the key? We test the charging system and replace what's failed.

A/C & Heating Repair

Georgia summers are no joke. We check refrigerant, compressors and blower motors so you're not stuck sweating it out.

General Auto Repair

Water pumps, radiators, exhaust, belts, sensors and the odd-job repairs that don't fit a neat category. Ask us.

Pre-Purchase & Used Vehicle Checks

Looking at a used truck or car? Have us look it over first so you know what you're actually buying.

Don't see what you need? That's normal — give us a call at (321) 246-9545 and describe what's going on with your vehicle.
